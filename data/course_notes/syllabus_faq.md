# Course Syllabus and FAQ

## Office hours
Office hours are Tuesdays 14:00-16:00 and Thursdays 10:00-12:00 in the Turing Building, room 3.14. No appointment is needed. Bring your code and the exact error message.

## Assignment deadlines
Assignments are due at 23:59 on the Friday of the week listed in the schedule. Submissions are through the course portal. Late work loses 10 percent per day up to three days, after which it is not accepted.

## Extensions
Extensions are granted for documented illness or personal emergency. Email the course coordinator before the deadline, not after. Heavy workload in other courses is not grounds for an extension.

## Grading breakdown
Assignments are 40 percent, the midterm is 20 percent, the final project is 30 percent and participation is 10 percent. You must pass the final project to pass the course.

## The final project
The final project is done in groups of three or four. You pick a dataset, state a question, build a model and write a four-page report. The report matters more than the accuracy number.

## Exam format
The midterm is 90 minutes, closed book, one double-sided page of handwritten notes allowed. It covers everything up to and including cross-validation.

## Academic integrity
Discussing ideas is encouraged. Sharing code is not. You must be able to explain every line you submit. AI assistants may be used for learning but any generated code must be declared in your submission.

## Required software
You need Python 3.10 or newer, a text editor and Git. Installation instructions are on the course portal. Lab machines have everything preinstalled.

## Getting help
Post questions in the course forum before emailing. Most questions have already been answered there. Include what you tried and what happened.

## Attendance
Lectures are recorded and posted within 24 hours. Labs are not recorded and attendance is part of the participation grade.

## Textbook
The textbook is recommended, not required. All examinable material is covered in lectures and the posted notes. A copy is on reserve in the library.

## Re-marking
Requests for a re-mark must be submitted within one week of grades being released and must explain specifically what you believe was marked incorrectly. The whole submission is re-marked, so a grade can go down as well as up.
